For shops

Ruko is a price comparison and price alert app for Android. This page explains what it reads from your site and how to ask us to leave your site out.

What Ruko reads

  • Public product pages that a Ruko user has chosen to track: product name, price, M.R.P., stock status, seller and image link.
  • Your public search results page, when a user searches for a product or compares prices across shops.

Ruko does not log in, does not add to cart, does not place orders, and does not read anything behind a login.

How it runs

  • Only on users' own phones. Each request comes from one user's phone, for that user's own tracked items or search, the way their phone's browser would.
  • Low frequency. Tracked products are checked at the times the user chooses, once a day by default, with pauses between requests to the same site. Ruko backs off automatically when a site says no.
  • No central server. Ruko has no server that collects, stores or resells your prices or catalogue. Each user's data stays on their phone.

Asking to be excluded

If you would rather Ruko didn't read your site, email [contact email to be confirmed] from an address at your company's domain, with your site's address. We will reply within 7 days. Once we agree, Ruko stops reading your site on every phone within about a day, without users needing to update the app.

You can use the same address to report a problem, for example if Ruko sends more requests than described above.
