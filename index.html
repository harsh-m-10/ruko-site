Ruko Privacy Policy

Ruko. Daam girega. (Wait. The price will drop.)

In short: Ruko has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. The developer does not collect any data about you. Everything Ruko knows stays on your phone.

What Ruko does

Ruko is a price tracker. You share or paste a product link, and Ruko checks that product's price and availability on shopping sites from your own phone, then notifies you when the price drops or the item comes back in stock.

Data stored on your phone

  • The products you track: links, titles, images, prices and price history.
  • Your settings: check schedule, alert thresholds and similar preferences.
  • The ntfy access token, if you enter one. It is kept in Android's secure, hardware-backed storage (Keystore) and is never included in exports or backups made by Ruko.

This data never leaves your phone except as described below. The developer has no copy of it and no way to see it.

Network connections Ruko makes

  • Shopping sites. Ruko fetches the product pages you track (for example Amazon.in or Flipkart), and the sites' own search pages when you compare prices, directly from your phone, the same way your browser would. Those sites see a normal request from your phone and handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Quick-commerce delivery location (optional). Sites like Blinkit or Zepto show prices for a delivery location. If you set one, you pick it on the site's own page inside Ruko's in-app browser. That site's cookies stay on your phone and are sent back only to that site. Ruko does not read or store your location.
  • ntfy (optional). If you set up an ntfy topic in Settings, Ruko sends your price alerts (item name, price and site) to the ntfy server you entered, so you can receive them on other devices.

There are no other network connections: no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to fetch product pages and, if you set them up, reach your chosen providers.
  • Notifications: to alert you about price drops and items back in stock.
  • Run at startup, keep awake, background work: so scheduled price checks can run in the background.
  • Vibrate: for alert notifications.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There is nothing to share: Ruko does not send your data to the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app to remove it and its history. Uninstalling Ruko removes all of its data, including stored keys, from your phone.

Children

Ruko is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
